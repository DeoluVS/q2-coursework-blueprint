How to Play...

  1. To start the game you press the start button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. A sequence will play after you clicked the start button.
  3. After the sequence is done showing itself, all the buttons will appear.
  4. You have to enter the right sequence.
  5. If you enter the wrong input the screen will flash yellow, orange or red depending on the level of accuracy.
  6. If you enter all right responses then the screen will flash green.

Additional notes!

  • If there are repeats in the sequence you will either see the button turn black on every other repeat. On early levels, there will be a number on buttons indicating repeat items in the sequence.

Good Luck!!!
